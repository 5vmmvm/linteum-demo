02 — THE CENTRE

Research, education
and outreach.

We are a non-profit cultural association interested in the historical figure of Jesus and the objects associated with him.

  • Research. We carry out our own studies and support those of other researchers.
  • Education. We help our members develop their knowledge of these subjects.
  • Outreach. We share knowledge with the public through publications and activities.
What is sindonology? +

Sindonology is the study of the Holy Shroud. Its name comes from the Greek words sindon, meaning linen cloth, and logia, meaning study or discourse.

HOW WE WORK

Impartiality,
as a starting point.

We study these objects without assuming their authenticity.

We seek to understand what these objects are and what they can tell us about Jesus of Nazareth. Relics offer a way to learn more about him as a historical figure.

The CES is a non-denominational organisation bringing together believers and non-believers of different ages and professions.
